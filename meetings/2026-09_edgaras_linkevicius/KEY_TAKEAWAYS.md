# Key takeaways - Edgaras forest-health research consultation

**Source:** The recovered transcript, not a formal biological assessment or a final Forest Internet design decision.

## Biological/physiological indicators discussed

- **Stem diameter and growth increment**: tree-specific responses potentially related to decline, but affected by size, site and ordinary growth.
- **Sap flow**: candidate tree-response information; Edgaras explicitly indicated uncertainty about whether it can distinguish beetle attack.
- **Resin response**: discussed as a reaction around beetle entry holes; no validated unattended resin-flow sensor was selected.
- **Temperature, rainfall, wind and other climate variables**: possible confounding factors and reference measurements, potentially from meteorological stations.
- **Pheromone traps**: candidate plot-scale beetle-pressure indicator, not a tree-specific confirmed attack label.
- **Defoliation/crown status**: possible field-observed response; climate and subjective visual scoring confound interpretation.
- **CO2, photosynthesis and advanced imaging**: discussed but practical attribution, costs and deployment complexity raise concerns.

## Experimental design and feasibility

Edgaras emphasized comparing affected and control trees that are comparable in species, age, diameter/size, soil, stand density, neighboring competition and related conditions. He suggested that around **15 trees** would be more meaningful for a preliminary study than only three, but did **not** provide a statistical power calculation or guarantee of inference quality. Repeated monitoring over time is important; the 15-month project duration may be short for biological questions.

Edgaras described his expertise as **forest growth and yield**, not direct bark-beetle research. He mentioned possible forestry institute contacts and long-term tree physiology/transpiration data, but explicitly did not confirm whether any such data contain beetle labels or would be available. Budget and instrument costs were important constraints, not verified quotes.

## Interpretation boundary

The professor did NOT specify the eventual BME688, Model 404, ADS1115, specific MCU/LoRa hardware, a finalized sample size, field plot, forest-pest diagnostic model or verified early-warning performance. Those are later design considerations, not decisions attributable to this meeting.
